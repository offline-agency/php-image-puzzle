## Neighbourhood on Packagist

Context only, not market size. Searches through
`https://packagist.org/search.json?q=<query>`:

| Query | Results | Observation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `image puzzle` | 9 | This package is the first result (3 installs); the others are unrelated image tools |
| `jigsaw` | 50 | Dominated by `tightenco/jigsaw`, a static site generator (about 475,000 installs), and its templates. Puzzle packages: `lhp/jigsaw-puzzle-verify` (a captcha, about 8,000 installs), `bywulf/jigsawlutioner` (a solver, 117 installs) |
| `puzzle` | 1,638 | Mostly unrelated (`puzzle/*` utility packages, captchas, sudoku); `robiningelbrecht/puzzle-generator` has 0 installs and 13 favourites |

Two findings to act on:

- "jigsaw" alone finds a static site generator first. Descriptions and
  tutorials should say "jigsaw puzzle", "image puzzle" and "sliding puzzle".
- Packagist users only see 1.0.0 until the first automated release, so any
  discovery before then leads to the oldest code.
